What this app is for

This app shows where your money goes in a typical month.

It reads your bank statements, groups payments that repeat, and lets you say what each group is for.

What you'll need

Several months, ideally a year, of statements for each current, savings and credit card account, saved as CSV files.

Most banks allow you to download your statements as CSV files.

Privacy

Everything stays in this browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.

This also means that opening the app in another browser app or on another computer won't carry over your data. The backup facility can be used for this.

Closing and reopening the app works just fine, though, and will preserve your data, even across reboots and browser sessions.

Backups are plain files and are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe.

The steps

  1. Import each account (you can drag and drop several statements for the same account at once).
  2. Confirm transfers between your accounts (optional).
  3. Categorise the biggest buckets until the progress looks good enough (either in Categories or on the Categorise screen).
  4. Read your Typical month.
  5. Back up.
  6. Later, import new statements and update your categories.
