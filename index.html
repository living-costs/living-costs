What this app is for

This app shows where your money goes in a typical month.

It reads your bank statements, groups payments that repeat, and lets you say what each group is for.

What you'll need

Several months of statements for each current, savings and credit card account, saved as CSV files.

Most banks allow you to download your statements as CSV files.

Privacy

Everything stays in this browser on your computer. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.

This also means that moving to another browser will not preserve your data, but you can close and reopen the app.

Backups are plain files and are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe.

The steps

  1. Import each account (you can import several statements for the same account at once).
  2. Confirm transfers between your accounts (optional).
  3. Categorise the biggest buckets until the progress looks good enough.
  4. Read your typical month.
  5. Back up.
  6. Later, import new statements and update your categories.
